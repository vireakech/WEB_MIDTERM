The Story

Rise above the game, every single match

LEGENDARY started with a simple idea: the gear you play in should be as bold as the way you play. What began as a single pair of match boots now covers the full range — from lightweight speed boots to control silhouettes built for the long game.

Every drop is inspired by the players we grew up watching, and every detail is tested on real turf before it ever reaches the pitch.

  • Six generations of boot silhouettes
  • Tournament-grade studs on every FG and SG outsole
  • Recycled knit uppers and low-impact packaging
